Child Safety Standards

Our commitment

A Falcon ("the App", "we", "us") has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. This applies to all users, drivers, riders, and any content, communication, or conduct that takes place on or through the App. We are committed to protecting minors and preventing the App from being used to facilitate child exploitation in any form, including sexual abuse imagery, grooming, trafficking, or solicitation.

Prohibited conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on A Falcon:

  • Uploading, sharing, storing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to sexually exploit a minor.
  • Using the App to facilitate trafficking or exploitation of a minor.
  • Any communication with a minor that is sexual in nature or intended to lead to abuse.
  • Creating, requesting, or sharing content that sexualizes minors, including computer-generated or altered imagery.

How we respond

When we become aware of a violation of this policy, we will:

  • Immediately remove the offending content and/or suspend or permanently ban the associated account.
  • Preserve relevant evidence as required by law.
  • Report confirmed or suspected CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or relevant local law enforcement authorities, as required by applicable law.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement investigations related to child safety.

How to report a concern

If you encounter content or behavior on A Falcon that you believe violates this policy, or that endangers a child, please report it immediately using the contact details below. Reports are reviewed by our designated child safety contact and treated as a priority.
